# Count-first web contract — 30 September 2026

Affordable quantity is the primary discovery signal; tenure follows, and percentage is supporting context. A useful quantity does not prove operative tenure, legal terms, product suitability or acquisition availability.

## Existing supported bounds

The web-only assessment already represents independently source-qualified exact, approximate, range, at-least and up-to quantities. It preserves source identity, passage, document date, application and named scope. The unchanged threshold kernel uses each search/buyer boundary; it has no universal 50-home minimum.

| Supplied qualified evidence | Minimum 70 | Minimum 75 | Maximum 74 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exact 72 | meets | does not meet | meets (whole-scheme only) |
| At least 70 | likely meets, bound displayed | investigate | investigate |
| Explicit range 71–74 | likely meets, range displayed | does not meet | likely meets (whole-scheme only) |
| Unresolved alternatives 71, 73, 74 | investigate | does not meet only if every scoped alternative fails | investigate |
| Legacy 72 without source/scope | unknown; reported lead retained | unknown | unknown |
| Unknown | unknown | unknown | unknown |

A component can support a scheme minimum but cannot establish the whole-scheme maximum. Separate applications/components are never added together. The approved ten-percent investigation band applies only to qualified approximate point figures without explicit bounds, calculated from the actual threshold; exact figures and source-defined bounds are not rounded or expanded.

## Revisions and the 71/73/74 example

Specifications 020 and 021 and the local claim selector at `8b651afd29d3c923bc5787934379be2a9736ac78` were reviewed. Specification 021 retains immutable source assertions and explicit reviewed CORRECT/SUPERSEDE links; dates alone never choose a winner. The selector retains pending or competing relevant assertions as alternatives, validates application-to-scheme scope, and fails safely when replacement review is invalid.

Credible 71/73/74 claims may support a qualified “70+ affordable homes reported” product lead after relevant scope, stage, completeness and revision handling are established. This web-only artifact cannot establish that review: it excludes the database-dependent claim selector, stores source-defined bounds only, and has no explicit provenance type for a derived consensus lower bound. Automatically rounding the smallest legacy/alternative number would erase that distinction. Therefore this release supports already evidenced lower bounds/ranges and keeps unresolved alternatives accessible for investigation; it does not manufacture a new 70+ assertion. A future claim-selection increment must preserve all contributing claim IDs and explicitly record the review basis for any derived conservative bound, rather than pretending a source said “70+”. Production migration/import remains separate.

## Presentation and verification

Legacy legal classifications display as “Reported AH status: …; operative terms unverified”. Count qualification is never legal-status verification. A source URL is displayed when supplied; document identity alone is explicitly labelled “source link unavailable”. Unqualified ranges/one-sided bounds receive the same unverified warning as legacy point counts and cannot qualify a numeric match. No production record is upgraded.

Focused tests cover source-defined lower-bound/range filters and exports, unresolved same-scope 71/73/74 claims, component and separate-application restrictions, missing source/scope, and link versus legal-status qualification. No matching-policy version, payload, source schema, stored evaluation, or threshold kernel changes are made by this presentation increment.
