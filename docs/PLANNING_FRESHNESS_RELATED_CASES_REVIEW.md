## Smallest increment proposed: bounded known-scheme revisit

Scope first: existing known planning schemes, Focus chain and Hyde negative controls, using existing portal adapters. No new general crawler, buyer-field implementation, live schedule or automatic AI refresh.

1. **Due selection:** propose weekly document-register and reference/relationship checks for active opportunities, unresolved AH/condition evidence and recent decisions; monthly for other monitored consented schemes. Changes/retryable failures get earlier bounded eligibility with backoff, not an unbounded immediate loop. Cadences are proposed configuration, not activated jobs or freshness guarantees. Use oldest successful-check age plus last attempt/backoff, rotate councils and reserve a small bounded revisit allocation per council so recovery/new discovery cannot consume every turn.
2. **Durable work:** use P0-A ownership/outcome/budget patterns; separately design revisit work keyed by council+application reference+stage/route. Persist attempt/success/error/deferred reason, next eligibility, listing/search continuation, route coverage and discovered child frontier. Deduplicate canonical references within council and document logical identities within application. Lease expiry/retry must not double-apply completed work. Review an additive schema contract before any migration; do not overload ParentLookupWork as proof of functionality it does not yet provide.
3. **Relationship discovery:** bounded query of each due verified family node, not only the oldest root; adapter-exposed related-case links plus existing citation searches as separately reported routes. Cap nodes/pages/time and carry the frontier forward. Inspect fetched identity/council and explicit citation before proposing a family edge. Preserve suggested/ambiguous links for review. Same site is not same scope; references to supersession/conditions are typed evidence, not guessed chronology.
4. **Document comparison:** compare listing identities/metadata, persist new candidates, and selectively revalidate decision notices, AH statements, amendments and legal details under bounded byte/time budgets. Same-URL changes need an explicit refresh path past identity/file-cache skips; compare bytes and normalised text, retain prior version rather than overwrite. Use ETag/Last-Modified only where supplied, with periodic bounded verification because unchanged metadata alone does not establish unchanged contents. Unchanged revisions must not re-extract/review repeatedly; failed PDFs remain inaccessible, never checked-no-change. Preserve issue date, portal publication date and observation/download time separately.
5. **Source-qualified review:** emit a proposed material-change record for total/AH count, tenure, planning stage or party role tied to document version/application/component. Reuse existing scoped claim-review concepts without auto-importing production claims. Hold contrary versions and decision-stage distinctions side by side. Document acquisition has no paid client; any later extraction/evaluation needs independent approval/budget. Retrieval of a granted notice may resolve legal terms; a statement or discharge register label alone cannot.
6. **Freshness display:** application × stage labels: last attempt, last successful complete check, new/revised documents, related routes/pages checked, uncovered routes, partial failure and deferred/oldest age. Example: 'Status checked; document check partial—decision PDF unavailable; related citation search complete for DC/085997 only, DC/093884 deferred.' Council/global views must show successful early councils alongside a failed later council; overall failure cannot erase completed work, and partial success cannot imply full coverage.
