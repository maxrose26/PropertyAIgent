## A. Charter and decision

**Master stage 4 — Bounded reliable pipeline, OPEN.** The master gate is supported collection, durable retention, screening, qualification and discoverable results with the required Greater Manchester reliability and freshness evidence. The **current workstream gate** is owner acceptance of this proposal and recording the implementation envelope. The **next gate** is a real Tameside collection, independent database readback, repeat and genuine record change. Only the owner advances the master roadmap.

The owner's 18 November audit brief selects separate collection, storage and refresh, with later read-only consumption by PropertyAIgent. Deliver a queryable register beginning on 1 January 2022 across all ten councils, including small, non-residential and subsequently amended applications. A buyer's strategy, residential threshold or AI score must never decide which references enter the register. Its commercial purpose is to give PropertyAIgent evidence it can repeatedly screen and reconcile as buyer strategies change. Buyer ranking, land ownership, AH, document extraction, notifications to prospects and a public search product remain with their respective workstreams.

**Decision recommended:** one independent Python collector service, one PostgreSQL database and a versioned SQL read contract. Start with Tameside, then prove Manchester's different portal family. Use the same repository for source control and independently release the new job. Accept one implementation envelope for the agreed sequence and spend. Existing product history stays in place.

**Date contract.** The archive has a fixed start of **1 January 2022**. At the audit cutoff (18 November 2026, 00:00 Europe/London), the initial baseline runs through **17 November 2026 inclusive**: 1,782 completed local dates or 17,820 council-days. Each subsequent London day extends the end through yesterday; January 2022 stays searchable. Store half-open intervals internally, translate to inclusive dates for the portal and track intervening days until the service goes live. Keep observed versions and show today's unfinished date separately if collected.

Membership follows each council's proved search basis in section B. An application received in range but unvalidated may be invisible on a validated-date register. Report that population as unassessed unless a complementary register/list reconciles it. A missing record-level date can still have evidenced membership of a filtered window. A decision date, publication time or first observation cannot stand in for the filter date. This delivers the observable official register within the declared semantics. Any requested broader claim about every received application requires the supplementary evidence.

## C. Proposed architecture and contract

**Runtime.** One scheduled Python command with three modes: discover a specified date window, refresh an exact-identity batch, or run the bounded scheduled allocation. One service-specific build target and released revision, independent secrets, database migrations, schedule, logs and restore procedure. Keep pure modules shared or move them behind compatibility imports only where the legacy caller still passes its regressions. Pin the first build to reviewed reader versions. Scope deployment to changes of the data-service package and its shared dependencies. A product release, suspension, failed screening or unavailable buyer database cannot stop collection. Instance/region outages can still affect co-located services.

**Hosting recommendation.** Use a dedicated Supabase Micro project for planning data in the organisation the owner expects to put on Pro. It has its own PostgreSQL instance, credentials, migrations and restore boundary. The shared organisation handles billing. Use Render for the scheduled Python collector and Playwright/Chromium, reusing the proven execution environment. Keep its database credential in the new job’s secret settings and away from browser processes. Use a nearby European region and a TLS connection to the new project. Choose the direct database route if supported by the worker network, or Supabase’s session pooler for IPv4. Verify the lock and readback behaviour during PD-01. The original all-Render proposal simplified networking with one provider and an internal connection. With Pro already funded for other projects, a separate Supabase project offers lower expected incremental cost and a familiar database platform. Keep raw evidence private and use versioned read-only views for the later PropertyAIgent connection.

**Minimum PostgreSQL design.** Keep payloads private. Store the exact public application row/detail representation consumed by the parser, or an exact bounded public evidence excerpt with location, encoding and a clear scope. Never label a normalized reconstruction or a hash as the original page. Strip session/form secrets from storage by selecting public record nodes before persistence; evidence requiring whole-response identity may retain a response digest and a separately labelled safe excerpt. List receipts retain applied criteria, visible total, ordered segments, page hashes and terminal evidence. Capture document metadata/links where available; document downloads, OCR and AI are outside this service.

**Write rules.** Commit observations, version/current pointers and successful checkpoint atomically for each bounded batch. Use a deterministic attempt/observation key to reconcile a lost acknowledgement; a fresh scheduled observation has its own identity. Lock the identity during compare-and-append. Equivalent field values update sightings and freshness without a new semantic version. Later reversal A→B→A creates version 3. A sparse listing cannot erase a detail value. An older observation arriving late remains history and cannot regress a newer accepted value. Store explicit corrections with provenance and preserve the prior literal. A partial window may retain individually proved rows; coverage advances only after full count, terminal and criteria validation. Keep field-level last-success times so a listing reread cannot refresh a decision last verified on detail. Separate raw/current counts, changed records, screening and published opportunities in every report.

**Consumer decision.** Start with SELECT-only SQL views; one internal consumer can join and page by stable keys without deploying/authenticating a second service. Tables can change behind v1. A small HTTPS API becomes worth its maintenance for external consumers, cross-network access constraints or independently enforced rate limits. For the initial change reader, use a committed change sequence allocated under the single writer transaction (with serialization retained on any future concurrency). Page by sequence, checkpoint only after consumer commit, and upsert by council/ref/version on replay. Expose deletes/corrections as explicit events if supported. A plain timestamp or uncoordinated serial ID alone can skip late commits. Keep private/source-only fields out of the contract. Contract-test with an isolated reader before acceptance; wire the actual PropertyAIgent consumer only after owner service acceptance.

### Running the register from January 2022

1. **Reserve current capacity first.** Run a single scheduled job, for example hourly in UTC once authorised. Rotate councils. Each day revisit the preceding seven complete London dates for every enabled council; revisit the preceding 28 completed dates across a seven-day cycle to catch late indexing. Run detail refresh independently of listing membership. Record the filter, range, caps and permitted council in the run before any request. First run after downtime queues every missed date. A late arrival in an older window must still be discoverable through a periodic older-window rescan or a separately proved publication feed.
2. **Partition history.** Seed ten ledgers at 1 January 2022. At the frozen cutoff, each council has 254 full weeks and a four-day remainder, or **2,550 initial council-windows** across all ten before splits, bridge work, repeats or detail checks. Upsert by council/date basis/interval. Prioritise current discovery and recent gaps while reserving backfill capacity for older years. Check an early 2022 week per council as soon as its collector is qualified: portal migrations can leave older material on a different official register. Record unavailable periods and their evidence. At 10 verified historical windows/day the initial scan needs at least 255 days; at 40/day, 64 days. Measure throughput, early-record availability and disk use before publishing a completion date.
3. **Handle caps and outages.** Retain an incomplete parent attempt. Split overflow into nonoverlapping children, first after the adapter can prove those shorter ranges. In the Tameside counted path this requires an explicit tested change. With a one-day overflow qualify finer official partitions with a disjoint/exhaustive union or a larger separately costed page allowance. Never skip a remainder or restart the whole council. For session-based pagers re-read the bounded window, since an old page token can point to a changed list. Detect repeated pages, changed total or sort, cross-window leakage and ambiguous end states. Positive totals, unique keys, contiguous pages, stable criteria and terminal proof are all required. A zero needs an explicit council-specific zero plus the applied criteria. Reconcile retained rows on partial attempts without issuing negative facts.
4. **Refresh older records.** Prefer exact detail on stored council/reference and validated source key. As starting service targets, recheck pending, unknown and ambiguous status every 7 days; for 90 days after a terminal decision recheck every 30 days; recheck older terminal records at least every 90 days across the archive. Keep older unresolved records on the pending schedule until resolved. Spread old window re-enumeration across a 90-day cycle for late publication/corrections even where a reference was never previously seen. Give recent omissions a short retry with backoff, respect Retry-After, cool down the individual council and continue others. A disappeared page, failed response or blank field does not establish withdrawal. A future official change feed may reduce polling after measured reconciliation. Existing contracts do not establish a complete updated-since cursor.
5. **Balance and measure.** Prioritise due discovery and oldest overdue detail, reserve a share of each healthy cycle for history (initial suggestion 50% of remaining requests), bound all lanes and report their independent lateness. Start with one in-flight operation per host, no automatic capacity escalation; reuse stricter known per-council pacing. On a 429 stop that council for at least the instructed cooldown. Treat an unexplained block as a gap. On loss of DB commit acknowledgement open a fresh read-only check using the operation key; retry only known-uncommitted work. Retain the last successful data and timestamp throughout outages.
