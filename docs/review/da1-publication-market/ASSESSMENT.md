## Decision

**C. PUBLICATION-FIRST DISCOVERY — NOT VIABLE as the primary Greater Manchester DA1 direction on the evidence obtained.**

This is an investment decision about the evidence available now, not a claim that useful publications do not exist. No council has a publication source qualified across provenance, currentness, completeness and commercial rights sufficiently to recommend implementation. There are two Tier B leads: Trafford's council-branded weekly PDF, accessible through a third-party archive, and Oldham's officially documented weekly email distribution. Neither establishes a ready primary feed. Committee publications are selective supplementary evidence. Most other advertised weekly lists are views of the planning portal, not independent distribution mechanisms.

Do not start another parser or portal implementation. Return to REVIEW for the higher-level acquisition decision: qualify a licensed structured supplier against the GM requirement, or separately authorise evidence/rights qualification of the best publication lead. Buying discovery coverage and reserving council evidence for selected verification remains a credible option, but this assessment has not qualified a supplier.

## O. DA0 fit and proposed checkpoint semantics — design only

Reuse PUBLICATION_LIST and COMMITTEE_PUBLICATION source classes; no schema/runtime change. Keep candidate states NON_OPERATIONAL/PROPOSED and capabilities UNPROVED pending all eight gates: authority, completeness, freshness/latency, identity, provenance, resource bounding, rights and schema stability. Do not mutate registry records in this slice.

Minimum identity remains council + exact application reference. Preserve issue identifier/date and whether its basis is received, registered, validated or decided. Never silently equate those dates. Existing APPLICATION_DISCOVERED and qualified source/decision change events are sufficient concepts. A future decision stream would be separately qualified under CHANGE_DISCOVERY/decision fact capabilities; this assessment does not add a DECISION_DISCOVERY enum or treat committee resolution as issued consent.

For a qualified weekly-file source, a checkpoint would identify accepted publisher issue + content digest, not merely download time. Recheck recent issues for corrections, reconcile duplicate council/reference identities, preserve prior issue provenance, and identify changed republished files. Exact overlap duration must follow observed late-publication behaviour, currently unknown. Missing issue or uncertain empty week prevents claiming continuous coverage; it is not evidence of zero applications. File-byte completeness and complete council-window coverage are separate controls. Never emit source removal/withdrawal just because a later publication omits an application. No checkpoint or scheduling implemented.

## P–S. Product decision and independent review

“Discover cheaply, triage, enrich selectively” is commercially credible when discovery is licensed and sufficiently complete. It could focus investment on PropertyAIgent's differentiated intelligence rather than planning-search parity. But “discover everything cheaply” is not established here. Exact reference alone allows deduplication, not reliable commercial triage; minimal type/location/proposal or another accepted relevance source would be needed eventually. Unknown relevance must not be silently excluded. No AI or matching work is authorised by this assessment.

The best unresolved lead is Trafford. Before any parser preflight, REVIEW would need an authoritative primary distribution route, current/archive and correction/empty-week evidence, period semantics and commercial reuse qualification. This is evidence qualification, not the recommended implementation slice. Under decision C, **no implementation slice is recommended**. Request a higher-level acquisition decision, with licensed national discovery coverage as a procurement/validation alternative. No provider is selected by this publication-only assessment.

Independent review completed by a separate reviewer against the full task and this artifact: GO for docs-only publication, no blocking revision. The reviewer agreed C is defensible and B would overstate demonstrated coverage; endorsed the mirror/policy/current-feed distinctions, completeness qualifications, rights scope and avoidance of the 211-reference denominator. One non-blocking recommendation was adopted: Tameside is described as an indexed official OGL notice with direct confirmation unavailable, rather than an unqualified rights confirmation. Review was of the evidence and reasoning recorded here, not an independent retrieval of linked council sources. No implementation or merge authority follows.
